Before you begin

  • Check with your doctor before starting any new exercise routine.
  • Keep a sturdy, non-wheeled chair nearby for balance and seated exercises.
  • Stop right away if you feel pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath.
  • Keep a glass of water within reach and take breaks whenever you need one.

    Content protection

    No website can truly disable a screenshot — the operating system owns that key. What this does: blocks printing and PDF export, blocks copying and dragging the content, blanks the screen the moment a capture shortcut is pressed, and stamps every screen with the viewer's name and ID so a leaked picture can be traced back.

    2. Application type: Web application.

    4. Copy the Client ID (it ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com) and paste it below.

    Payments & ownership

    Return links to paste into Stripe

    In each Stripe Payment Link, open After payment → Redirect customers to a page you choose and paste the matching link below. Access only switches on when the customer comes back through one of these.

    Saved.

    How the money reaches you.Every purchase now goes through Stripe's own checkout page: the customer types their card there, and the money lands in the Stripe account that owns the Payment Links above. This page never sees a card number and never unlocks anything on its own. The one honest limitation of a site with no server: the unlock happens because Stripe sends the customer back to your return link, which a determined person could type by hand. When you are ready for airtight verification, add a small backend that listens to Stripe webhooks.
